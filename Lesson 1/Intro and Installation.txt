OpenCV
-------
OpenCV (Open Source Computer Vision Library) is an open-source computer vision 
and machine learning library. 
It allows us to process images and videos, detect objects, faces and even handwriting


Why Learn OpenCV?
------------------
1.Comprehensive Image Processing: 
        OpenCV has a range of functions to manipulate and 
        analyze images helps in making it ideal for various applications.
2.Real-Time Video Processing: 
        It supports video capture and real-time video processing.
3.Cross-Platform: 
        Works on multiple platforms like Windows, Linux, macOS and Android.
4.Open-Source: 
        It is free to use and has a large community support.
5.Integration with Deep Learning: 
        It integrates with popular deep learning libraries like TensorFlow and PyTorch.

-------------
