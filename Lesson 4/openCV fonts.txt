OpenCV's built-in fonts are limited.

You can replace your font argument with any of the following:

cv2.FONT_HERSHEY_SIMPLEX
cv2.FONT_HERSHEY_PLAIN
cv2.FONT_HERSHEY_DUPLEX
cv2.FONT_HERSHEY_COMPLEX
cv2.FONT_HERSHEY_TRIPLEX
cv2.FONT_HERSHEY_COMPLEX_SMALL
cv2.FONT_HERSHEY_SCRIPT_SIMPLEX
cv2.FONT_HERSHEY_SCRIPT_COMPLEX


Bonus: To Make the text italic Add cv2.FONT_ITALIC using the bitwise OR operator (|):
Example - 
font = cv2.FONT_HERSHEY_COMPLEX_SMALL|cv2.FONT_ITALIC
